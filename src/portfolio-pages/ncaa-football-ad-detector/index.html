What it does

When you're juggling multiple college football games at once, the question is always the same: which one should I be watching right now? This tool answers it.

It lists the games in priority order, showing each one's quarter and status, whether it's currently in an ad break and when it's expected to return. Just look for the highest game that isn't in an ad.

Keep the tab open and in front so your browser doesn't throttle it in the background, which would hurt its ad-break predictions.
