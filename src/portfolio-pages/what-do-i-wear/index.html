01 / 09

What Do I Wear01

Pick a lane

Two big buttons: Wardrobe to manage your clothes, and Dress to get told what to wear. There's a Clear button for starting fresh.

What Do I Wear02

Build your wardrobe

Start with clothing categories like shirts and pants. Each row has a green refresh button and a red remove button, and the pink plus adds another category.

What Do I Wear03

Shoes, hats, anything

Categories are yours to add: shirts, pants, shoes, hats. Whatever you actually wear, the app can pick from it.

What Do I Wear04

Your clothes, in photos

Open a category to see every item as a photo, and add new pieces with the plus button. No guessing which grey shirt is which.

What Do I Wear05

Tell me what to wear

Hit the button and the app picks a shirt and pants for you. No more standing in front of the closet.

What Do I Wear06

The full outfit

Add shoes and a hat to the mix and you get a whole look in a single tap.

What Do I Wear07

Not feeling it?

Tap again for a different combination. The app does the choosing, so you can skip the deciding.

What Do I Wear08

Laundry warnings

Run low on a category and the app says so: “Your Pants are running low, please do your laundry soon. You only have 1 clean one left.”

What Do I Wear09

Behind the menu

The overflow menu holds Settings, Do Laundry, and the Shirt and Legwear queues, so it keeps track of what's clean.
