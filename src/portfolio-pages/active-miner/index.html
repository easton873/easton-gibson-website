DEPTH 01/10

LEVEL 01

Title screen

Active Miner opens on a stark black, red and green menu: Play, Leaderboards or Settings. Dig deep, then see how you stack up.

LEVEL 02

Surface level

You start on the surface as a stick-figure miner. Up top sit the shop, an upgrade arrow and settings. Below, the dirt is packed with gold and blue ore.

LEVEL 03

Tunnels & ladders

Dig down and the ground opens into tunnels. Ladders let you climb back out of everything you've carved away.

LEVEL 04

Digging in

Mine block by block with a live readout of depth, durability, cash, stone destroyers, highscore and level. Here the chicken skin is hard at work.

LEVEL 05

Stone in the way

Grey stone blocks and pockets of ore make every tunnel a little different, so you plan a route and keep an eye on your durability.

LEVEL 06

Map view

A zoomed-out map shows the whole dig at once, tunnels snaking through the ground, with arrows to scroll up and down.

LEVEL 07

Cash out

Back at the shop you can sell what you've mined, one at a time with Sell or everything at once with Sell All.

LEVEL 08

Level up?

Levelling up is a gamble. It erases the world and resets your stats to level 1, so the game makes you confirm first.

LEVEL 09

Pause menu

Resume, change your skin, or Save and Quit. The red button makes it clear which one ends your run.

LEVEL 10

Pick a skin

Retro, Default, Chicken, Astronaut or T-rex. Pick whichever miner you want to dig with.
