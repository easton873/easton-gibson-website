STAGE 01

Join the fight

Pick a name, pick a colour, hit Join Game. Multiplayer Trevor is an online RPG built with Node.js and socket.io, where everyone who joins shares the same live world.

Node.jssocket.io

STAGE 02

Spawn in

You arrive as a wizard with a magicbolt, 300 health, 100 mana and a pile of gold. Enemies are already wandering the grass.

STAGE 03

Room to room

The dungeon is a chain of rooms linked by wooden doors. Each one is packed with enemies that all attack in their own way.

STAGE 04

Spell slots

Your spells are numbered, and the one you've equipped is highlighted. Here a ring of fire closes in around a pack of enemies.

STAGE 05

Afterimage

Afterimage splits off copies of the wizard while cyan bolts fly across the room. Picking the right spell at the right moment is the whole game.

STAGE 06

Fireball

A fireball streaks toward a cluster of enemies, with health bars floating above anything that's been hit.

STAGE 07

More magic

Firewall, freeze, summon turret and more. With 20+ moves to unlock, you build a toolkit that suits how you play.

STAGE 08

The Teacher

Gold from defeated enemies buys new moves from the Teacher: fireball, landmine, lightning, chain lightning and the 2000 gold armageddon.

STAGE 09

The Upgrader

Already own a spell? Spend gold to boost its damage, range, duration or cost, and make your favourites even better.

STAGE 10

Game over

Get overwhelmed and it's YOU DIED. Respawn, spend what gold you've got and jump back in.
