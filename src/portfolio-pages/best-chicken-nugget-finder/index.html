01

Meet the chicken

Best Chicken Nugget Finder opens on a glowing lime chicken. It's a Flutter app built to settle the only debate that really matters: whose nuggets are the best?

Flutter

02

Two big buttons

The home screen keeps it simple: edit what you judge nuggets on, or jump straight to the restaurants.

03

Your rules

Smell, cool, temp, crispiness, colour. Criteria are yours to define, and each row has a menu to edit or delete it.

04

Weighted scoring

Name a criterion and set its weight, meaning how much it matters compared with the others. Crispiness can count for more than colour.

05

The leaderboard

Every restaurant gets a score out of five. McDonald's, Subway and Chic Fil a are all in the running, each with a Rate button.

06

Rate and calculate

Pick a score for each criterion, hit Calculate, and the weighted average appears. Chic Fil a lands at 3.8.

07

Everyone's a critic

Switch between tasters from the side drawer, so every person keeps their own ratings and their own opinion on nuggets.
